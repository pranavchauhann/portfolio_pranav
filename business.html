Chauhan Enterprises

Chauhan Enterprises, founded by brothers Pranav and Naveen Chauhan, is a GST-registered Proprietary Firm committed to transparent, sustainable, and high-quality metal recycling. We bridge industries and the environment by turning scrap into valuable resources, driving a cleaner and greener future.

Our Mission

Our mission is to lead the transformation towards a cleaner, greener, and more sustainable future. We are committed to revolutionizing the recycling and waste management industry by implementing innovative solutions that conserve resources, reduce environmental impact, and promote the principles of a circular economy. We strive to empower businesses and communities to make environmentally responsible choices by providing reliable, efficient, and eco-friendly services — ensuring that today's actions create a healthier planet for generations to come.

Our Values

● Integrity & Transparency: Operating with honesty, accountability, and openness in every interaction.
● Environmental Responsibility: Taking active measures to protect and preserve our natural world.
● Commitment to Quality: Delivering exceptional service and exceeding client expectations.
● Client-Centric Approach: Placing our clients' needs at the heart of everything we do.
